        How to play

        Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be a real word. After you guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were.

        crane

        Green: right letter, right place.

        slate

        Yellow: in the word, somewhere else.

        house

        Grey: not in the word.

        The daily word is the same for everyone and changes at midnight UTC. Practice gives you a fresh word whenever you want one.
